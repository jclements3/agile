Title: Latest line per key
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: keep the best record per key
Timebox: 15
Kind: stdio

Input: lines "YYYY-MM-DD ID status", in any order.
Output: for every ID, its most recent line (the latest date; on equal dates
the line that came later in the input), sorted by ID.

Examples
  2026-02-01 K-1 open / 2026-03-01 K-1 shut   ->  2026-03-01 K-1 shut
  2026-01-01 Z a / 2026-01-01 Z b             ->  2026-01-01 Z b
