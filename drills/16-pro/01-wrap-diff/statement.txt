Title: Bearing difference on a circle
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: lessons
Pattern: modular arithmetic: the shorter way round
Timebox: 5
Kind: function
Call: wrap_diff($a, $b) -> $degrees

Return the angle between two bearings in degrees, the shorter way round the
360-degree circle: always between 0 and 180. Bearings may be decimals and
may be outside 0..360.

Examples
  wrap_diff(350, 10)  ->  20
  wrap_diff(180, 0)   ->  180
  wrap_diff(0.5, 359) ->  1.5
