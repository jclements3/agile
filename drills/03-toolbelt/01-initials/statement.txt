Title: Initials
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: lessons
Pattern: split, map, join
Timebox: 3
Kind: function
Call: initials($full_name) -> "A.B.C."

Return the upper-case first letter of every word of the name, each followed
by a dot. Words are separated by any run of spaces.

Examples
  initials("ada king lovelace")  ->  "A.K.L."
  initials("grace")              ->  "G."
