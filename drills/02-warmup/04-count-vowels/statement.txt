Title: Count vowels
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: warmup
Pattern: character counting
Timebox: 3
Kind: function
Call: count_vowels($s) -> $count

Count the characters of $s that are one of a, e, i, o, u, in either case.
Y is not a vowel here.

Examples
  count_vowels("Programming")  ->  3
  count_vowels("AEIOU")        ->  5
  count_vowels("")             ->  0
