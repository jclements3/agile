Title: Product of the others
LeetCode: 238. Product of Array Except Self (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: prefix and suffix products
Timebox: 15
Kind: function
Call: product_except_self(\@nums) -> [...]

Return a list whose element i is the product of every element of @nums except
the one at i. Do it without division, in O(n).

Constraints: n >= 2; integers, zeros allowed; products fit in an integer.

Examples
  product_except_self([2, 3, 4])     ->  [12, 8, 6]
  product_except_self([5, 0, 2])     ->  [0, 10, 0]
