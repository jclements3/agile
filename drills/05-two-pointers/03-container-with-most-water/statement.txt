Title: Most water between two walls
LeetCode: 11. Container With Most Water (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: two pointers, move the shorter wall
Timebox: 15
Kind: function
Call: max_water(\@heights) -> $area

Wall i stands at position i with height heights[i]. Choose two walls; the
water they hold is (distance between them) * (the shorter height). Return the
largest such amount, in O(n).

Constraints: n >= 2; heights >= 0.

Examples
  max_water([2, 8, 3, 7, 1])  ->  14      (walls 1 and 3: 2 * 7)
  max_water([4, 4])           ->  4
