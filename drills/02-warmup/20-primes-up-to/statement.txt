Title: Sieve of Eratosthenes
LeetCode: 204. Count Primes (similar; the primes themselves here)
Origin: warmup
Pattern: sieve
Timebox: 6
Kind: function
Call: primes_up_to($n) -> [ primes <= n ]

Return every prime less than or equal to n, ascending, using a sieve: mark
the multiples of each prime as composite. Be ready to say why crossing off
can start at p * p.

Examples
  primes_up_to(20)  ->  [2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19]
  primes_up_to(2)   ->  [2]
  primes_up_to(1)   ->  []
