Title: Most common word
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: lessons
Pattern: counting with a tie-break
Timebox: 4
Kind: function
Call: most_common_word($text) -> $word

Return the word (whitespace-separated) that occurs most often; on a tie,
the alphabetically smallest. The text has at least one word.

Examples
  most_common_word("the cat the dog the")  ->  "the"
  most_common_word("b a a b")              ->  "a"
