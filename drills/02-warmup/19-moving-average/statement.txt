Title: Moving average
LeetCode: 346. Moving Average from Data Stream (similar; a whole list here)
Origin: warmup
Pattern: sliding window sum
Timebox: 5
Kind: function
Call: moving_average(\@xs, $k) -> [ averages ]

Return the average of every window of k consecutive values, in order.
len(xs) >= k >= 1. The averages are plain numbers (1.5, 2.5, ...).

Examples
  moving_average([1, 2, 3, 4], 2)  ->  [1.5, 2.5, 3.5]
  moving_average([1, 1, 1], 3)     ->  [1]
  moving_average([5], 1)           ->  [5]
