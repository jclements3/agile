Title: Reverse the digits
LeetCode: 7. Reverse Integer (similar; no overflow rule here)
Origin: warmup
Pattern: string and number conversion
Timebox: 3
Kind: function
Call: reverse_digits($n) -> $m

Return the integer whose digits are those of $n in reverse order. A negative
number stays negative; trailing zeros of $n disappear (120 gives 21).

Examples
  reverse_digits(123)  ->  321
  reverse_digits(-45)  ->  -54
  reverse_digits(120)  ->  21
