Title: Fewest coins
LeetCode: 322. Coin Change (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: bottom-up dynamic programming
Timebox: 20
Kind: stdio

Input: line 1 holds the positive coin values; line 2 is an amount >= 0.
Output: the fewest coins (any value may be reused) adding up exactly to the
amount, or -1 if no combination does. Amount 0 needs 0 coins.

Greedy is a trap: with coins 1 5 6 and amount 10, 6+1+1+1+1 uses five coins
but 5+5 uses two.

Examples
  1 5 6 / 10  ->  2
  4 / 6       ->  -1
