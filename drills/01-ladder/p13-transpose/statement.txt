Title: Transpose a matrix
LeetCode: 867. Transpose Matrix (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: parse a matrix
Timebox: 5
Kind: stdio

Input: line 1 is "R C"; then R lines of C integers each.
Output: the transpose: C lines of R integers, space-separated.

Examples
  2 2 / 1 2 / 3 4   ->  1 3 / 2 4
  1 3 / 5 6 7       ->  5 / 6 / 7
