Title: Caesar cipher
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: warmup
Pattern: modular arithmetic on character codes
Timebox: 5
Kind: function
Call: caesar($s, $k) -> $shifted

Shift every letter k places forward in the alphabet, wrapping from z to a,
keeping its case. Anything that is not a letter is unchanged. k >= 0.

Examples
  caesar("abc", 2)            ->  "cde"
  caesar("XYZ", 3)            ->  "ABC"
  caesar("Hello, World!", 13) ->  "Uryyb, Jbeyq!"
