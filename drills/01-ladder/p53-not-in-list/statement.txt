Title: In one list, not the other
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: hash set difference
Timebox: 15
Kind: stdio

Input: a first list of ids, one per line, then a line holding only ---,
then a second list of ids.
Output: the ids of the first list that are not in the second, in
first-list order, each once; or a single line "-" when there are none.
Blank lines are ignored.

Examples
  X-1 / X-2 / --- / X-1   ->  X-2
  K / --- / K             ->  -
