Title: Merge two sorted lists
LeetCode: 21. Merge Two Sorted Lists (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: dummy head, splice the smaller front
Timebox: 10
Kind: function
Call: merge_two_lists($a, $b) -> $head

Both lists (nodes { val, next }) are sorted ascending. Return one sorted list
made by relinking their nodes (no new value nodes needed). Equal values may
come in either order. Either list may be empty.

Examples
  merge_two_lists(1 -> 4 -> 6, 2 -> 3)  ->  1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 -> 6
  merge_two_lists(undef, 5)             ->  5
