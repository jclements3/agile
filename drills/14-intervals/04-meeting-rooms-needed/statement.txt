Title: How many rooms?
LeetCode: 253. Meeting Rooms II (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: sweep sorted starts against sorted ends
Timebox: 15
Kind: function
Call: min_rooms(\@meetings) -> $n

Each meeting is [start, end) (half-open: a meeting ending at 10 frees its room
for one starting at 10). Return the fewest rooms that hold every meeting.

Examples
  min_rooms([[0, 30], [5, 10], [15, 20]])  ->  2
  min_rooms([[7, 10], [2, 4]])             ->  1
  min_rooms([])                            ->  0
