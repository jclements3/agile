Title: Longest run without repeats
LeetCode: 3. Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: sliding window
Timebox: 10
Kind: stdio

Input: one line of lowercase letters, possibly empty (length up to 10^5).
Output: the length of the longest contiguous stretch in which no letter
appears twice.

Examples
  pwwkew  ->  3
  aaaa    ->  1
  (empty) ->  0
