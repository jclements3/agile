Title: Percent with one decimal
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: lessons
Pattern: sprintf formatting
Timebox: 2
Kind: function
Call: percent($part, $whole) -> "75.0%"

Return part / whole as a percentage with exactly one decimal place and a
percent sign. whole is not zero.

Examples
  percent(3, 4)  ->  "75.0%"
  percent(1, 3)  ->  "33.3%"
  percent(2, 3)  ->  "66.7%"
