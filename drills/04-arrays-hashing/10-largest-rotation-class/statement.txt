Title: Largest rotation class
LeetCode: 796. Rotate String (similar; this groups many strings)
Origin: mock
Pattern: canonical form as a hash key
Timebox: 15
Kind: function
Call: largest_rotation_class(\@words) -> $size

Two strings are equivalent when one is a rotation of the other: moving some
leading characters to the end turns one into the other ("abcd" and "cdab").
Group @words into equivalence classes and return the size of the largest
class. No words -> 0.

Examples
  largest_rotation_class(['abcd', 'cdab', 'bcda', 'abdc'])  ->  3
  largest_rotation_class(['a', 'a', 'b'])                   ->  2
