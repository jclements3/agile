Title: Running median
LeetCode: 295. Find Median from Data Stream (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: two heaps: low half (max) and high half (min)
Timebox: 25
Kind: function
Call: running_median(\@stream) -> [medians]

Read the values in order and, after each one, record the median of all
values so far (for an even count, the mean of the two middle values).
Return the list of medians. Re-sorting after every value is too slow for
10^5 values.

Examples
  running_median([5, 15, 1, 3])  ->  [5, 10, 5, 4]
  running_median([2, 2])         ->  [2, 2]
