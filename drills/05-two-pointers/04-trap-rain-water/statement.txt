Title: Trapped rain water
LeetCode: 42. Trapping Rain Water (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: two pointers with running maxima
Timebox: 25
Kind: function
Call: trapped_water(\@heights) -> $units

Columns of width 1 have the given heights. After rain, water sits above a
column up to the lower of the tallest column on its left and the tallest on
its right. Return the total units of water held. O(n) time, O(1) space.

Examples
  trapped_water([3, 0, 2, 0, 4])  ->  7
  trapped_water([1, 2, 3])        ->  0
