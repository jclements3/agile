Title: Day numbers
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: dates to day counts
Timebox: 15
Kind: stdio

Input: line 1 is the date of day 1 (YYYY-MM-DD); every further line is a
date.
Output: for each further date, "Dn" where the day-1 date itself is D1, the
next day D2, and so on; a date before day 1 prints "before".

Examples
  2026-03-01 / 2026-03-01 / 2026-03-31   ->  D1 / D31
  2026-03-01 / 2026-02-28                ->  before
