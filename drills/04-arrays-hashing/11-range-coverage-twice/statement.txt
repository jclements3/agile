Title: Covered at least twice
LeetCode: 1094. Car Pooling (similar; same difference-array idea)
Origin: mock
Pattern: difference array and prefix sum
Timebox: 15
Kind: function
Call: covered_twice($n, \@ranges) -> $count

Items are numbered 1..$n. Each range [a, b] (inclusive, 1 <= a <= b <= n)
covers items a to b. Return how many items are covered by two or more ranges.
n can be 10^6 with 10^5 ranges: O(n + q), not O(n * q).

Examples
  covered_twice(10, [[1, 5], [4, 8], [5, 5]])  ->  2    (items 4 and 5)
  covered_twice(5, [])                         ->  0
