Title: Balanced brackets
LeetCode: 20. Valid Parentheses (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: stack
Timebox: 10
Kind: stdio

Input: line 1 is n; then n strings made only of the characters ()[]{}.
Output: for each string, YES if every bracket is closed by the matching
kind in the right order, else NO.

Examples
  3 / {[]} / [(]) / ))   ->  YES / NO / NO
