Title: From Roman numerals
LeetCode: 13. Roman to Integer (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: warmup
Pattern: one pass with a look-ahead
Timebox: 5
Kind: function
Call: from_roman($numeral) -> $n

Turn a valid Roman numeral back into an integer. A symbol worth less than the
symbol after it is subtracted (IV is 4); otherwise it is added.

Examples
  from_roman("MCMXCIV")  ->  1994
  from_roman("LVIII")    ->  58
  from_roman("III")      ->  3
