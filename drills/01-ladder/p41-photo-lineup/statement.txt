Title: Two-row lineup
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: sort both, compare pairwise (exchange argument)
Timebox: 25
Kind: stdio

Two groups of the same size n pose for a photo: group 1 in the back row,
group 2 in the front row. In every column the back person must be strictly
taller than the front person. Each row may be put in any order.

Input: line 1 holds group 1's heights; line 2 holds group 2's.
Output: YES, then the back row ascending, then the front row ascending, if
some arrangement works; otherwise NO.

Examples
  7 4 / 3 6      ->  YES / 4 7 / 3 6
  5 5 / 5 1      ->  NO
