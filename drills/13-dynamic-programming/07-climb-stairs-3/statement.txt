Title: Stairs, 1 to 3 steps at a time
LeetCode: 70. Climbing Stairs (similar: there the step is 1 or 2)
Origin: lessons
Pattern: memoized recurrence
Timebox: 10
Kind: function
Call: climb3($n) -> $ways

Count the different ways to climb n stairs when each move climbs 1, 2 or 3
stairs (order matters: 1+2 and 2+1 differ). Zero stairs can be climbed one
way: by doing nothing.

Examples
  climb3(4)  ->  7
  climb3(1)  ->  1
  climb3(0)  ->  1
