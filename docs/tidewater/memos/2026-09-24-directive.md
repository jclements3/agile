# Tidewater Coastal Observation Program

## TW-PM

## 24 September 2026

## Memorandum for Tidewater Systems Engineering

## From: Tidewater Program Manager

## Subject: Adoption of DevSecOps Practices for Systems Engineering Artifacts

Effective FY27, program systems engineering artifacts, including the system model, will be developed and maintained using DevSecOps practices on the enterprise DevSecOps platform (GitLab).

The Tidewater system model will be transitioned from SysML v1 to SysML v2 textual notation and placed under configuration control in the enterprise GitLab instance. The prime contractor's SysML v1 deliveries under CDRL A003 remain the contractual baseline until further notice; the government will maintain a derived SysML v2 model and keep it current with each delivery.

Systems engineering leads will demonstrate:

- automated quality checks on every model change;
- traceability from DOORS requirements to the model, computed rather than hand-maintained;
- peer review and approval of every change, with a complete audit record;
- reproducible baselines;
- measurable progress reported at regular intervals.

The Tidewater MBSE lead will brief initial results at the December program management review.

Tools and data flows require ISSM approval before use with program data.

Training resources are the enterprise platform's documentation and a recorded webinar. The point of contact in the enterprise DevSecOps office is listed on the platform's home page. Teams are encouraged to start small and iterate.
