Title: Reverse the words
LeetCode: 151. Reverse Words in a String (similar; a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: split, reverse, join
Timebox: 5
Kind: stdio

Input: one line with at least one word; words are separated by runs of
blanks, and the line may start or end with blanks.
Output: the words in reverse order, separated by single spaces.

Examples
  one two three        ->  three two one
  "  up   down "       ->  down up
  word                 ->  word
