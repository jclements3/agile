Title: Pairs within budget
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: sort, then two pointers
Timebox: 10
Kind: stdio

Input: line 1 is a budget t; line 2 holds n integers, unsorted, possibly
negative.
Output: how many index pairs i < j have x[i] + x[j] <= t.

Constraints: n up to 2*10^5. The answer does not depend on order, which
licenses a sort.

Examples
  6 / 5 1 2 4   ->  3
  0 / 1 1       ->  0
