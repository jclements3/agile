Title: Running median
LeetCode: 295. Find Median from Data Stream (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: two heaps
Timebox: 20
Kind: stdio

Input: line 1 is n; then n integers, one per line.
Output: after each integer arrives, the median of all integers so far, with
exactly one decimal place. With an even count the median is the mean of the
two middle values.

Constraints: n up to 10^5: re-sorting after every arrival is too slow.

Examples
  3 / 5 / 1 / 3      ->  5.0 / 3.0 / 3.0
  2 / -2 / 7         ->  -2.0 / 2.5
