Title: Paths across a grid
LeetCode: 62. Unique Paths (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: grid DP, one rolling row
Timebox: 10
Kind: function
Call: unique_paths($rows, $cols) -> $n

Moving only right or down, how many different paths lead from the top-left
cell to the bottom-right cell of a rows x cols grid? Both are at least 1.

Examples
  unique_paths(3, 3)  ->  6
  unique_paths(1, 5)  ->  1
