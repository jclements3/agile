Daily workflow and weekly schedule

A suggested calendar for the solutions architect role at the kit's actual operating scale (10 teams, ~100 people), derived from the tempo in WORKFLOW.html and from sim/daily-job-sim.pl, a standalone time-accounting simulation of the role (not part of the tested kit in lib//bin//tests/).

1. What the simulation shows

Run it with:

perl sim/daily-job-sim.pl [--teams N] [--people-per-team N] [--seed N]

It models one full two-week sprint, day by day, costing every activity in minutes of the architect's own time: the daily townhall (fixed 15 min), the pipeline run (paste chat, run chat/answers/compile/report/tree — a few minutes of keyboard time; the Perl processing itself is sub-second regardless of scale), flag triage (each blocked/silent/stuck flag costs time to read and decide, not to compute), and the sprint's fixed events (planning, mid-sprint health check, refinement, review/retro).

The headline result at 10 teams / 100 people: process overhead averages roughly 60 minutes a day beyond the daily townhall, leaving on the order of 7 hours a day for deep architecture work. The two heavy days are Day 1 (planning) and Day 14 (review/retro/close) — those are where the calendar needs real blocks, not the daily grind. Triage time scales with flag rates, not raw people count, which is why the joint-townhall model holds up at 100 people: you read flags, not 95 raw answers.

The rates baked into the simulation (how often a team has a blocker, goes silent, or raises a cross-team ask) are illustrative priors. Once a sprint or two of real data exists, replace them in team_flags()/cross_team_asks() with observed rates for a forecast instead of a guess.

2. The daily rhythm (every workday, identical regardless of where in the sprint it falls)

08:15  daily.pl cards            member cards out before the call
08:30  Townhall                  fixed 08:30-08:45, all teams, one Teams call
08:45  Pipeline + triage         paste chat -> :SChat -> :SAnswers -> :SCompile -> :SReport -> :STree
                                  read the FLAGS, not the raw answers -> :SDraft
09:00  Protected deep-work time  the rest of the day, except the sprint-cycle events below

Everything from 09:00 onward is protected deep-work time except the four sprint-cycle events above (Planning, Health-check, Refinement, Review) — those are the only things that should ever get a scheduled meeting stacked on top of the townhall, per the anti-pattern list in WORKFLOW.html §12: "Stand-ups that become status reports to you. Your inputs are verbs; the conversation is theirs."
