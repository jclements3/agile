Title: Clamp with defaults
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: lessons
Pattern: default arguments, min/max
Timebox: 3
Kind: function
Call: clamp($x, $lo, $hi) -> $y      ($lo defaults to 0, $hi to 10)

Pin x into the range [lo, hi]: below lo gives lo, above hi gives hi,
otherwise x. lo and hi are optional, defaulting to 0 and 10.

Examples
  clamp(15)        ->  10
  clamp(-3)        ->  0
  clamp(5, 0, 4)   ->  4
