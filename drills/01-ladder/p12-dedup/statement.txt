Title: De-duplicate, keep order
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: seen-hash filter
Timebox: 5
Kind: stdio

Input: one line of zero or more tokens separated by spaces.
Output: the first occurrence of each token, in input order, joined by
single spaces. An empty line gives an empty line.

Examples
  red blue red green blue  ->  red blue green
  z z                      ->  z
