= Part IV: Making the case

Part III followed the team through twelve weeks, one sprint at a time. Part IV steps back and asks the question
management asked on 24 December: was it worth it? The answer has to be built from measurements the team agreed
before the work started, computed by the system rather than typed into slides, and honest about what did not move.

The chapters run in the order Jim prepared them. [[analytics]] shows the views GitLab offers for free and what
each one says about a model team. [[scorecard]] puts the twelve measures from [[directive]] side by side, before
and after. [[proving]] answers the question every port must answer: is the v2 model complete and correct?
[[briefing]] is the briefing itself, slide by slide, with the demo script. [[communicating]] is the weekly rhythm
of reports that made the briefing a summary rather than a surprise. The last chapters of the part turn to the
people: how the team changed its mind, a day in the life at week twelve, and how documents still matter.

Everything quoted here comes from `docs/tidewater/`, the evidence of the simulated run. Where this part says a
number, it cites the file it came from.
