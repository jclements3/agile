Title: The k points nearest the origin
LeetCode: 973. K Closest Points to Origin (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: max-heap of size k by squared distance
Timebox: 15
Kind: function
Call: k_closest(\@points, $k) -> [[x, y], ...]

Each point is [x, y]. Return the k points closest to (0, 0), in any order.
Compare squared distances (x*x + y*y): no square roots needed. The tests
have no ties at the boundary.

Examples
  k_closest([[1, 3], [-2, 2]], 1)          ->  [[-2, 2]]
  k_closest([[3, 3], [5, -1], [-2, 4]], 2) ->  [[3, 3], [-2, 4]]
