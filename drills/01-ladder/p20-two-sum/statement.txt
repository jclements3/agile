Title: Two sum, stdin to stdout
LeetCode: 1. Two Sum (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: hash map of value -> earliest index
Timebox: 10
Kind: stdio

Input: line 1 is a target t; line 2 holds n >= 2 integers x[0..n-1].
Output: "i j" (0-based, i < j) with x[i] + x[j] = t. Among all such pairs
pick the smallest j, then the smallest i. Print "none" if no pair works.

Constraints: n up to 10^5, so the double loop is the trap.

Examples
  10 / 4 9 1 6   ->  1 2
  2 / 1 1 1      ->  0 1
  5 / 1 1        ->  none
