Title: Group anagrams
LeetCode: 49. Group Anagrams (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: hash of a canonical key -> list
Timebox: 10
Kind: function
Call: group_anagrams(\@words) -> [[...], ...]

Two words are anagrams when they use the same letters the same number of
times. Split @words into groups of mutual anagrams and return the groups as a
list of lists. The order of the groups and of the words inside a group does
not matter.

Constraints: lowercase words, possibly empty strings; up to 10^4 words.

Examples
  group_anagrams(['ten', 'net', 'cat', 'act', 'dog'])  ->  [['ten','net'], ['cat','act'], ['dog']]
  group_anagrams([''])                                ->  [['']]
