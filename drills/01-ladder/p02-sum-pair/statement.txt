Title: Sum a pair
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: parse and compute
Timebox: 2
Kind: stdio

Input: one line with two integers a and b, each between -10^9 and 10^9.
Output: a + b.

Ask why the bounds are stated: in a language with 32-bit int the sum can
overflow. Perl's integers are 64-bit here.

Examples
  10 5      ->  15
  -4 -6     ->  -10
