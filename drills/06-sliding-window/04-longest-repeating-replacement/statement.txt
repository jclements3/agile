Title: Longest run after k replacements
LeetCode: 424. Longest Repeating Character Replacement (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: sliding window, length minus the top count
Timebox: 20
Kind: function
Call: longest_after_replacements($s, $k) -> $length

You may change at most $k characters of $s (uppercase letters) into any other
letter. Return the length of the longest run of one repeated letter you can
make.

Examples
  longest_after_replacements('ABAB', 2)     ->  4
  longest_after_replacements('AABABBA', 1)  ->  4
  longest_after_replacements('', 3)         ->  0
