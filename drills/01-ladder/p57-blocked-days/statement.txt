Title: Blocked days per task
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: open/close events per key
Timebox: 15
Kind: stdio

Input: events "YYYY-MM-DD ID block" or "YYYY-MM-DD ID unblock" in date
order, then a last line "today YYYY-MM-DD".

Output: every task that was ever blocked, sorted by ID, as "ID days": the
total days blocked over all its stretches. A stretch ends at its unblock
date (same day = 0 days) or, if still open, at today. Add " !" when any
single stretch lasted more than 5 days.

Example
  2026-01-01 T-1 block / 2026-01-09 T-1 unblock / today 2026-01-10  ->  T-1 8 !
