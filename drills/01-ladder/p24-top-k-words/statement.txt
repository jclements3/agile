Title: Top k words
LeetCode: 692. Top K Frequent Words (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: hash counting, two-level sort
Timebox: 10
Kind: stdio

Input: line 1 is k; every later line (to end of input) holds words
separated by whitespace. At least k distinct words appear.
Output: k lines "word count", most frequent first; equal counts in
alphabetical order.

Examples
  1 / x y x       ->  x 2
  2 / q p / p q   ->  p 2 / q 2
