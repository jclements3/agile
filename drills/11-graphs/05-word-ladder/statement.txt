Title: Word ladder length
LeetCode: 127. Word Ladder (a pointer only: the statement below is this kit's own)
Origin: technique
Pattern: BFS over one-letter changes
Timebox: 25
Kind: function
Call: ladder_length($begin, $end, \@words) -> $n

Change $begin into $end one letter at a time; every intermediate word and
$end itself must be in @words ($begin need not be). All words are lowercase
and the same length. Return the number of words in the shortest such
sequence, counting $begin and $end, or 0 when there is none.

Examples
  ladder_length('cold', 'warm', [qw(cord card ward warm word wore)])  ->  5
  ladder_length('ab', 'cd', [qw(ad)])                                ->  0
