Title: Most common character
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: ladder
Pattern: hash counting with a two-level sort key
Timebox: 5
Kind: stdio

Input: one non-empty line of lowercase letters.
Output: the most frequent letter and its count, "c n". On a tie for the
highest count, choose the letter that comes first in the alphabet.

Examples
  mississippi  ->  i 4
  baab         ->  a 2
  qrs          ->  q 1
