Title: The calculator
LeetCode: 227. Basic Calculator II (similar; parentheses, no division here)
Origin: warmup
Pattern: recursive descent parsing
Timebox: 25
Kind: function
Call: evaluate($expr) -> $value

Evaluate an integer expression with +, -, * and parentheses, spaces allowed
anywhere. * binds tighter than + and -; operators of equal strength go left
to right. No unary minus, no division. Say the grammar before coding:

  expression := term (('+' | '-') term)*
  term       := factor ('*' factor)*
  factor     := NUMBER | '(' expression ')'

Examples
  evaluate("2*(3+4)-5")  ->  9
  evaluate("10-2-3")     ->  5
  evaluate(" 12 * 2 ")   ->  24
