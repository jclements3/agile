Title: Rectangle area
LeetCode: none (an original problem)
Origin: warmup
Pattern: arithmetic
Timebox: 2
Kind: function
Call: rect_area($width, $height) -> $area

Return width times height. Either side may be a decimal or zero.

Examples
  rect_area(3, 4)    ->  12
  rect_area(2.5, 4)  ->  10
  rect_area(0, 99)   ->  0
